# Event checklist

## Before you leave
- Charge the phone to 100% and bring a second charger and cable.
- Bring a phone stand or tripod, a USB cable long enough to reach power, and the booth PC charger.
- Install the latest AFAQ Booth Camera APK on the phone. Test everything once at home.
- Remove the phone case if the phone gets warm.

## Placement
- Put the phone at face height on a stand, back camera facing the guests, plugged into power.
- Leave air around the phone. Keep it out of direct sun.
- Light the guests from the front. Avoid a bright window behind them.

## Launch order
1. Turn on the phone hotspot (or join the chosen network).
2. Open the AFAQ Booth Camera app. Leave it in front with the screen on. Camera permission: allow.
3. Connect the booth PC to the same network.
4. Start the booth with `npm run dev`.
5. Gear, PIN, Camera source: Phone app, type the address shown on the phone, Test connection.
6. Take one test photo and one strip. Check the picture is upright and mirrored the way you want.

## Operator PIN
Gear, enter the current PIN (default `1234`), then Change operator PIN, type 4 digits and Save PIN.

## Custom stickers
Gear, PIN, then drop images onto the Custom stickers box or press Add. Transparent PNGs look best.

## If the preview freezes
1. Wait 10 seconds. The booth reconnects on its own.
2. Check the phone app is open in front and the screen is on.
3. Check the address in the app matches the booth setting (press Test connection).
4. Close and reopen the phone app.
5. Last resort: reload the booth page.

## Fall back to another camera
Gear, PIN, Camera source: This computer's camera, then pick the webcam or Iriun from the Camera list.
